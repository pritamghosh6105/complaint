# West Bengal Administrative & Geospatial Location Dataset

**Platform:** CivicPulse AI — State-Wide Citizen Complaint & Issue Management Platform  
**Coverage:** 100% of West Bengal State (23 Districts, Urban Local Bodies, and Rural CD Blocks)  
**Data Sources:**
1. **Local Government Directory (LGD)** — Ministry of Panchayati Raj, Govt. of India (https://lgdirectory.gov.in)
2. **Department of Panchayats & Rural Development (P&RD)** — Govt. of West Bengal
3. **Urban Development & Municipal Affairs Department (UD&MA)** — Govt. of West Bengal
4. **Census of India — Administrative Atlas of West Bengal**
5. **West Bengal Police Directorate** & Police Commissionerates (KP, Bidhannagar, Barrackpore, Howrah, Asansol-Durgapur, Siliguri, Chandannagar)
6. **India Post** — National Pincode Directory (West Bengal Postal Circle)
7. **OpenStreetMap (OSM)** — Boundary coordinates & centroids

**Compilation Date:** September 2024 / Live Update 2026  
**License / Usage:** Public Administrative Reference Data for Civic Governance

## Administrative Divisions Summary
West Bengal is divided into 5 Administrative Divisions comprising **23 Districts**:
- **Presidency Division:** Kolkata, North 24 Parganas, South 24 Parganas, Howrah, Nadia
- **Medinipur Division:** Paschim Medinipur, Purba Medinipur, Jhargram, Bankura, Purulia
- **Burdwan Division:** Purba Bardhaman, Paschim Bardhaman, Birbhum, Hooghly
- **Malda Division:** Malda, Uttar Dinajpur, Dakshin Dinajpur, Murshidabad
- **Jalpaiguri Division:** Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Jalpaiguri, Alipurduar, Cooch Behar

## Urban Local Bodies (ULBs)
- **7 Municipal Corporations:** Kolkata (KMC), Howrah (HMC), Asansol (AMC), Durgapur (DMC), Siliguri (SMC), Chandannagar (CMC), Bidhannagar (BMC).
- **120+ Municipalities** distributed across districts.

## Rural Local Bodies (Panchayati Raj)
- **342 Community Development (CD) Blocks** (Panchayat Samitis)
- **3,340 Gram Panchayats (GPs)**
- **40,000+ Mouzas & Inhabited Villages**
